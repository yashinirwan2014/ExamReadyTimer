Everything in one place

Tools for the whole study routine.

From planning your week to checking whether your remaining syllabus fits the time before an exam, ExamReadyTimer keeps the essentials connected.

🧠

Exam Readiness Gap Engine

Match upcoming exams to pending chapters, estimate the hours required and compare them with your available study capacity.

⏱️

Study-Time Ledger

Record completed focus sessions by subject and chapter, with a persistent local history.

🧠

Adaptive Daily Planner

Prioritise pending chapters using exam urgency, available study time and recent subject activity.

🔁

Spaced Revision Scheduler

Queue 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30-day reviews, then advance the next interval when you complete a review.

🧯

Recovery Planner

Spot earlier timetable blocks that are still uncompleted and decide what to reschedule.

⌛

Exam Countdown & Reminders

Add exam dates, see how many days remain and receive a saved reminder when your chosen reminder window begins while the planner is open.

🍅

Pomodoro Timer

Set study and break intervals, pause when needed and complete sessions at your pace.

🗓️

Study Timetable

Build a weekly plan and edit or remove study tasks as your schedule changes.

📚

Syllabus Tracker

Organise subjects and chapters, then mark topics pending or completed.

📈

Progress Analytics

See overall and subject-wise completion at a glance.

🎯

Focus Mode

Start a dedicated focus session with a clear countdown and simple controls.

💾

Backup & Restore

Create a ZIP backup of your exam dates, timetable, syllabus progress, study history and revision schedule.

🌍

Curriculum Search

Find subjects across the included curriculum labels using the tracker filters.

🧭

Student-first design

Simple windows and practical tools, designed to keep your study workflow organised.

Your work stays yours

Your study data is stored locally.

ExamReadyTimer stores your editable study files in your operating system's user data folder. Your progress is not tied to a hosted account.

Back up your files before moving to another computer or reinstalling the app.
